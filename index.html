Introduction

At FDI, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard information across the various websites, games, and digital services we develop.

1. Information We Collect

Because we build a wide range of projects, the information we collect may vary depending on the specific service you are using:

  • Information You Provide: Details you submit directly, such as your name, email address, usernames, or content provided via contact forms or user accounts.
  • Log and Usage Data: We use AWS to host our services, which may automatically log your IP address, browser type, and access times.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how users engage with our websites and apps. This may include your navigation path, time spent on pages, and device information.
  • Authentication & Backend Data: We use Firebase for services such as user authentication, database management, and crash reporting. This may involve collecting device identifiers and authentication tokens.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, maintain sessions, and analyze traffic patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Operational: To provide, host, and maintain the websites, games, and systems we develop.
  • Improvement: To troubleshoot technical issues, monitor system performance, and enhance the functionality of our services.
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries, provide support, or notify you of updates.
  • Personalization: To tailor experiences within our games or services based on your preferences.

3. Sharing of Information & Third-Party Partners

We value your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We share information with third-party service providers solely to enable them to perform services on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics: Used for traffic analysis. Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): Used for backend services and analytics. Firebase Privacy Standards.
  • AWS (Amazon Web Services): Used for cloud hosting and data storage. AWS Privacy Policy.

These third-party providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing services to FDI.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, or in response to a valid legal process, or if FDI undergoes a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect the integrity and security of the data we handle. While we strive to protect your information, please be aware that no system or internet transmission is entirely secure.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access the information we have collected about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our technology or legal requirements. We will post the updated version on our website with a revised "Last Updated" date.
